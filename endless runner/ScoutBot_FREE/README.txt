SCOUT-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series)
=======================================

Scout-Bot, a small sci-fi punk explorer robot, with idle and walk animations
in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/scout_idle.png, scout_walk.png          rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                            same with ground shadow
  sheets/scout_shadow.png                        shadow alone
  sheets/scout.json                              frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                    shared series palette

The full Robot Series Pack 1 adds:
  - Scout-Bot scan, hit and death animations
  - Miner-Bot (tracked miner with drill) and Drone-Bot (hovering quad-rotor), 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
